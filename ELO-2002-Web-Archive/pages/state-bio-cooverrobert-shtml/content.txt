Robert Coover Robert Coover is widely regarded as one of America's most influential living writers, author of some fifteen groundbreaking books of fiction, including Pricksongs &amp; Descants, The Public Burning, and most recently Ghost Town . Coover has for the past decade been teaching experimental courses in hypertext and multimedia narrative at Brown University. His 1992 essay on hypertext in the New York Times Book Review , "The End of Books," galvanized electronic literature fans around the world. This speaker delivers a keynote on April 5th .
