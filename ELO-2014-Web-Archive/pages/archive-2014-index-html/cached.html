The annual ELO Conference and Media Arts Festival includes peer-reviewed academic presentations, live performances, and an exhibition which showcases the latest artistic and scholarly work from our international community. Conference events must follow the policies established in the ELO Code of Conduct.

Please consider joining us for our upcoming conference and explore our conference archive below, many of which provide open access materials and recordings from each event.
