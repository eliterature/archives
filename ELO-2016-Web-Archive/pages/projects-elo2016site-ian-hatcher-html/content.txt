Drone Pilot 0.4 2015-16 Live performance + projections (html/javascript) Video trailer/documentation (if applicable): https://vimeo.com/145887878 Artists’ Statement: Drone Pilot is a performative reading concerned with telepresence, overflow, privacy, and systemic violence. Artist’s Bio: Ian Hatcher is a text/sound/code artist, author of Prosthesis (Poor Claudia, 2016), and developer of the poetry app Abra , a collaboration with Amaranth Borsuk and Kate Durbin. His code-inflected vocal performances have been presented widely in the United States and Europe. He lives in Brooklyn.
