Holiday Inn — rate increase for new reservations This is just to let you know that if you have not made your reservation at the Holiday Inn, College Park, you will no longer be able to get the $89 rate. Rooms at that rate are no longer available, and the hotel has increased its prices as well. The new flat rate is $159 — be sure to ask for the U. of MD rate, which is now $143.10. If you have further questions about this, please contact helen DeVinney.
