Every drop of Jameson whiskey sold worldwide is made in Cork, in a town called Midleton about 20 minutes outside the city. Delegates are invited to join a tour of the distillery, where they will enjoy amazing grounds as well as come face-to-face with the largest pot still in the world. In addition, delegates can also choose to take part in a tutored whiskey tasting of Jameson’s four premium whiskeys. Entry to the excursion will be strictly limited to guests who have added a ticket to their registration. Tickets can be purchased via the registration page: https://conference.ucc.ie/electronic-literature-organization-conference-and-media-arts-festival/elo/Site/Register
