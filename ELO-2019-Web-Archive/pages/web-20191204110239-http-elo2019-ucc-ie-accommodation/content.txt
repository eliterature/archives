University College Cork is a 10-minute walk from the centre of Cork city, so delegates are welcome to seek out their own accommodation. Furthermore, there are numerous B&amp;Bs situated on the Western Road which runs adjacent to the UCC campus. As the conference will be taking place during the busy summer months, delegates are advised to book early. A number of rooms, which will be assigned on a first-come-first-served basis, have been held at the following establishments for those looking to attend ELO 2019. Please follow the instructions provided for your chosen provider. We would ask that, where possible, the cheaper campus accommodation is left available for independent scholars, artists, and students who might be attending the conference with limited or no institutional support.
