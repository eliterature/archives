Registration is now open for delegates wishing to attend the 2019 Electronic Literature Organization Conference &amp; Media Arts Festival, to be hosted at University College Cork, Ireland. Registration Fees Standard €250 Student / Under-employed €150 Registration fees include annual membership of the Electronic Literature Organization . Registration Details Anyone who wishes to attend or participate in any aspect of the conference and/or media arts exhibition must register via the following secure service: https://conference.ucc.ie/electronic-literature-organization-conference-and-media-arts-festival/elo/Site/Register Please note: Delegates who have been accepted to the ELO2019 conference or exhibition must register by June 1st, 2019. The schedule for the conference will not be finalised until after this date, and any submissions by unregistered delegates will be removed from the program. Registration for delegates looking to attend but not present / exhibit at the festival will close on July 1st, 2019. Delegates who wish to purchase tickets for the conference banquet and/or excursion to the Jameson Whiskey distillery may do so during registration. Please note that entry to the banquet/excursion will not be permitted without a ticket. There is limited availability at both events so please book early to avoid disappointment.
