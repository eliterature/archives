The ELO2019 banquet will be hosted at Cork city’s Clayton Hotel, which overlooks the River Lee and City Hall. Guests will enjoy a welcome refreshment on arrival at the venue, before sitting down to a three-course meal accompanied by various official ELO proceedings, such as the announcement of the organisation’s annual prizes. Entry to the banquet will be strictly limited to guests who have added a ticket to their registration. Tickets can be purchased via the registration page: https://conference.ucc.ie/electronic-literature-organization-conference-and-media-arts-festival/elo/Site/Register
