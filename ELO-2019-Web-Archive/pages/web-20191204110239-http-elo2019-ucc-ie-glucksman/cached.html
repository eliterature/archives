Exhibition Space

#ELOcork’s e-lit exhibition will be hosted at the Glucksman Gallery, a prestigious national institution of the contemporary arts.

www.glucksman.org

A major landmark within UCC’s architectural landscape, Lonely Planet describes the Glucksman Gallery space thus: “A startling construction of limestone, steel and timber. Three floors of galleries display the best in both national and international contemporary art and installation.”
