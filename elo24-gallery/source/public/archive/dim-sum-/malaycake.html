Malay Cake

by Ka Lee Wong 黃嘉莉

馬拉糕

 [Cantonese] /   maa5 laai1gou1/ literal. Malay cake. dim sum.

Hong Kong is an international food paradise: it is so international that we have Swiss chicken wing, Mexican bun, Malay cake, toast a la francais, and so on and on. As a kid, I thought my hometown was awesome – I was just so proud of its metropolitanism in food – until I found out Swiss chicken wing did not come from Switzerland, Mexican bun not from Mexico, Malay cake not from the Malaya, toast a la francais pas de France! “Why so many lies about food?!” The young me was so frustrated (“yes lies but so tasty”). Later I came to understand these food lies weren’t malicious at all – they were local imaginations in the colonial era before Hong Kong actually became a metropolitan (so to speak, these were “poor men’s happiness”!). That time, the taste of the loi lo (“loyal”, meaning overseas, which is a Cantonese way to pronounce “royal”) was not affordable – imported ingredients were rare and expensive! Just like Malay cake: back to those days, a local chef wanted to make Malayan style pandan cake after he tried it in a fancy Western hotel. It was so good that he wanted to make these for his people. But it was just that he could not find pandan herbs at all in the city! The chef did not give up (I’m glad!) What did he do in the end? He just made a version of pandan cake without using any pandan herbs. Okay la, use your imagination! Just think about the Malaya when you eat the Malay-less cake; the imagination makes it taste great!


香港號稱國際美食天堂，國際到我哋有瑞士雞翼、墨西哥包、馬拉糕、法蘭西多士（仲有好多好多國際級野食名）。細個嗰陣一直都覺得家鄉嘅國際飲食文化好勁揪，好以佢為榮。點知！最後發現根本瑞士冇瑞士雞翼，墨西哥冇墨西哥包，馬拉冇馬拉糕，法蘭西冇法蘭西多士！「做乜講大話啫」？」細路時期嘅我諗唔開(「雖然係大話，不過都係好好食。」）。係到咗大個左先知道呢啲假嘢係殖民時代、香港經濟起飛之前、物質匱乏嘅年代嘅豐富想像（咪就係所謂嘅「窮人恩物」）。舊時「來佬」嘅味道唔係人人負擔得起 ，因為啲入口食材又難搵又貴！就好似馬拉糕咁啦，嗰個年代，有個本地廚師曾經係間好靚嘅酒店食過好正嘅馬拉斑蘭蛋糕，一試難忘，佢就好想自己整比啲街坊食，但係就搵極都搵唔到班蘭葉。不過廚師冇放棄自己，咁佢最後點算？佢咪自己整咗一款無班蘭葉嘅斑蘭蛋糕囉。OK啦，大家比啲想像力！當你食馬拉糕嘅時候諗吓啲馬拉風情，咁就自然有馬拉味㗎啦！
