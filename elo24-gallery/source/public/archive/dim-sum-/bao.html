Bao Bao 包飽

by Angela Chang 陳 欣 至

Capturing feelings before they flee
These words what do they mean to me
Sweet and savory things mixed in
Filling spaces in between
With dim sum tones, grab the syllables
Stuffing the lot, like they’re heroes.
Sek bao, Sek Bao you say aloud
Tasty flavors I must savor now.

Warmed on the inside
Singsong rhymes
Sliding over lips
Smiling as an idea flips
Pulling smiles from slips
Folded into creases, Into cranes.
Sending out good vibes, Pieces like paper planes.

The sweet desserts of simple joys
in everyday voices
A treat to my sight.
Moving to symphonies.
Sounding so bright
Blending syntactic sugars
Oh audible delights.


在感情逃離之前捕捉感情
呢啲說話對我意味住乜嘢
混合咗甜味同鹹味嘅嘢
填充兩者之間嘅空格
用點心音調，抓住音節
塞滿地段，就好似佢哋係英雄一樣。
食包食飽,你大聲說
我而家必須品嚐美味嘅味道。

內部温暖
歌頌押韻
滑過嘴唇
微笑，因為一個想法翻轉
從紙條中拉出笑容
拗成摺痕，變成起重機。
發出良好嘅共鳴，紙飛機噉嘅碎片。

簡單快樂嘅甜蜜甜點
在日常聲音中
對我嘅視力係一種享受。
轉向交響樂。
聽起嚟好明亮
混合句法糖
哦，聽覺上嘅喜悅。
