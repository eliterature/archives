Ham Sui Gok

by Lai-Tze Fan 范麗慈

The three paths of this salty river intersect here.
The four brothers meet and decide how to divide the land.

The first brother, the oldest, need not state his case.
He smokes a cigarette quietly with a sad and stiff lip.

The second brother wags a finger, the smallest of the group.
He wants for his mother, the only one born in Hong Kong.

The third brother is placid like the current of the river.
His fire will burn gently, his land lit with candles.

The fourth brother was born only after the war ended.
He alone ate enough, yet was the first to go.

The land will be divided and then given back to the people.
This family’s tears forge the salty stone in my throat.

鹹水角

呢條鹹水河嘅三條河道喺呢度交匯
四兄弟聚埋一齊，諗緊點樣瓜分啲土地

大哥唔使陳述佢自己嘅理由
佢擔住一口煙，扁住嘴

二哥手郁郁咁指控
佢係唯一一個喺香港出世，佢淨係想要佢阿媽

三哥睇落並冇波湧
佢嘅心火將輕款咁燃燒，心田點滿蠟燭

四哥係唯一一打仗後出世嘅
佢一個人食飽就第一個走咗

啲地會分返俾人民
呢個家庭嘅淚水喺我嘅喉嚨中滴成一嚿鹹鹹嘅石頭
