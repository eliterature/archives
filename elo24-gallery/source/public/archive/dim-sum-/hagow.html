Har Gow and Siu Mai

by Angela Chang 陳 欣 至


Four har gows filled with pink prawn,
elegant, oval, pearly whites.
Tender skin, closed and secretive—
a translucent and enticing sight.
So delicate, and harmonious.
Defy chopsticks poking slight.
Siu mai filled with seasonings.
Open-faced flower-shaped and bright.
Bold, round, adventurous fellows
stand in bamboo baskets like knights.
Tightly wrapped in armor, so yellow,
my tastebuds in anticipation excite.
With char siu buns and egg tarts here
are classic tastes of Canto bites.
Bao is pleasing, soft, and filling,
Tart's a thrilling golden spotlight.
Four Kings, one bowl, two pieces, tea.
Invite more and split the bill right.


蝦餃

四隻蝦餃、裏邊粉紅色
白色晶凝寶貝。
滑滑軟軟薄皮，
珍珠光芒綻月光光。
好味好靚
筷子輕輕戳一下。

燒賣好香味道、
開花盆清新。
大膽勇敢真誠朋友
好似軍隊企竹盆。

包得緊緊黃、
口想試想試。
有叉燒包、有蛋撻
香港運桔出名。

包好軟、好嫩，好飽
蛋撻好似金燈
四王一碗、兩件、飲茶。
请朋友食饭、唔好搶單。
