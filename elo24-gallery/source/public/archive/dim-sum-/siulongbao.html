Siu Long Bao

by Lai-Tze Fan 范麗慈

The siu long bao must have 18 delicate folds.

  1. The inventor of siu long bao sold them in his first store
        next to the classical Guyi Garden in Nanxiang, Shanghai.
  2. The Garden is 21 kilometers from the city center;
        the dumplings are measured to be 21 grams exactly.
  3. Five grams of dough kneaded by hand (just use feeling),
        16 grams of filling, no more no less.
  4. How do you get the soup into the dumpling?
        Make them in a cold environment with warm hearts.
  5. Keep the hands cold too, so not to melt the filling,
        but keep your language flowing like silken soup.
  6. The four of us work around a circular table,
        representing the West, North, South, and East.
  7. Our Eastern zodiacs are fire, water, air, no earth.
        Instead, we are grounded by the fullness of our bellies.
  8. The women fold their stories over 18 gestures,
        the dough is pinched to seal in our thoughts.
  9. We try tongue twisters in Cantonese and Mandarin,
        laughing over the tangle of our mother tongues.
  10. Admiring our scripts across multiple languages,
        admiring our handwriting in multiple coloured pens.
  11. Black coffee is called "veg caf" in Cantonese
        —I learned this through immersion and practice.
  12. Growing up as Asian women, it feels like we're not listened to.
        Our ideas are not always validated with our identities.
  13. Angela made her husband "a Martha Stewart dinner":
        roast beef and popovers, which she'd never tried before.
  14. I would like to make my lover a mackerel and radish stew.
        I hope it will be raining so he can stay with me all night.
  15. The 90s eyebrow was very unforgiving, Biyi agrees,
        and even worse are the bald patches left over.
  16. We spread Kalee's lo poa baeng across the Northeast,
        distracted by firecrackers and children dressed as dragons.
  17. This work cannot be done neatly, in fact.
        Dumplings taste best when folded with love.
  18. Our hands know the art of 18 delicate folds
        and we feed each other well from small bamboo baskets.


小籠包

小籠包必須有18個精巧嘅皺摺：
  1. 小籠包發明者第一間賣包嘅舖頭喺上海南翔古猗園旁邊
        上海南翔古猗園旁邊
  2. 古猗園距離市中心廿一公里
        啱啱好小籠包嘅重量都係廿一克
  3. 五克手工搓成嘅麵團（憑感覺）
        16克嘅餡，唔多唔少
  4. 點樣將啲湯汁灌入小籠包入邊呢？
        要喺凍嘅環境入邊，心要熱嘅
  5. 手都要凍，咁樣餡料就唔會化開，
        行文流暢到好似啲湯咁絲滑
  6. 我哋四個圍住張圓枱
        代表咗東南西北
  7. 我哋嘅五行係金木水火，就係冇土
        但係食飽、常滿係我哋嘅命格
  8. 我哋呢班女人用多過十八般嘅手勢，摺疊我哋嘅故仔
        搣住麵團，包起思緒
  9. 我哋係咁用廣東話同國語講急口令
        喺我哋各自打撠嘅母語中哈哈大笑
  10. 細味我哋用多種語言寫嘅字體
        細味我哋用唔同顏色嘅筆寫嘅字跡
  11. 黑咖啡喺廣東話叫「齋啡」
        呢個係我喺現實生活中浸淫番黎
  12. 喺亞裔女性嘅成長過程中，我哋嘅心聲，冇人聽得入耳
        我哋嘅想法，因為身份而不被接納
  13. 欣至整左餐Martha Stewart晚餐比佢老公
        燒牛肉同埋空心酥餅（其實佢都係第一次整）
  14. 我想為我嘅情人整一餐鯖魚燉蘿蔔
        我希望外邊落大雨，佢就可以成晚陪住我
  15. 90年代嘅眉毛好蝦人畫，璧伊都噏晒頭
        最衰就係會有啲甩毛嘅地方
  16. 我地分左嘉莉啲老餅婆餅喺張枱面嘅東北角
        但比出邊嘅炮竹同埋打扮成龍嘅細路搞到分晒心
  17. 事實上我哋做緊嘅嘢唔可能係完美嘅
        用愛摺疊嘅包，就係最正
  18. 我哋對18種摺疊嘅技術了然於心，
        我哋以竹蒸籠中嘅心意餵養彼此
