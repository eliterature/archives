Wifecake

by Ka Lee Wong 黃嘉莉

老婆餅

 [Cantonese] /   lou5 po4 beng2/ literal. Wife cake. pastry.

What’s wrong with Hong Kong pastries? There is no pineapple in pineapple buns, no piglet in piglet buns, and of course no wife in wife cake (“What do you want from street food?”). Hai la hai la, all you need is imagination – imagining the sweet romance behind the sweet pastry makes anything taste good. My mom is familiar with the story behind wife cakes – of course, we are from Yuen Long, where wife cakes are from, so we feel obliged to pass this on! (By the way, people who don’t know Yuen Long keep calling our town “the Great Northwest” because it is far. Please, please do not just think we hang out with cattle – we are not that rural!) Going back to the story of wife cake, my mom said wife cakes were created by this man who wanted his wife to come home. The wife did not run away, but she sold herself as a servant to a rich family so she could raise money to help her sick father. The husband wanted to get the wife back so badly, so he tried everything he could. At last he used what he could get – winter melon paste, sugar, lard and flour – and created this very sweet pie. He made lots and lots of pies every day so he could earn more money to get his wife back home. Was there a happy ending? Nobody knows! I hope they did, otherwise this cake won’t be a sweet pastry any more, but a pastry of broken hearts.


香港啲包點究竟搞乜鬼？菠蘿包入邊冇菠蘿，豬仔包入邊冇豬仔，老婆餅入面冇老婆！ （「街頭小食黎㗎，你想點啫？」）係啦係啦，我哋要俾多啲想像力！就諗吓啲包點入邊嘅甜蜜愛情故事啦，咁食咩都好食啲。我阿媽細個嗰陣同我講過一個老婆餅嘅傳說。梗係啦，我哋係本地元朗人，元朗就係老婆餅嘅發祥地，係元朗人都會應該知嘅！（講開又講，元朗成日俾人話係好遠嘅「大西北」，大家唔好以為淨係得牛，其實真係冇牛，邊有咁鄉下呀！）話番轉頭，阿媽話老婆餅係由一個好想老婆返屋企嘅男人整出嚟嘅。佢老婆冇走佬，只不過係佢為咗醫佢病咗嘅老竇而將自己賣身比個有錢佬做妹仔。個老公真係好想個老婆返嚟，佢絞盡腦汁，用僅有嘅材料（冬瓜蓉同糖，仲有麵粉同豬油），整咗個好好食嘅甜餅。佢日日整整整，係咁整，就係想賺多啲錢贖佢老婆返嚟。結果係點？佢地有冇有情人終成眷屬？其實真係冇人知，不過我都係想佢哋真係有團圓嘅！如果唔係食老婆餅就食唔出甜味，食心碎嘅味道就唔好啦！
