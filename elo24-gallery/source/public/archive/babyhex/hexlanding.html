After two decades in the biotech wilderness for alleged crimes against nonhumanity that were really all just one big misunderstanding,
Infodome Inc. is proud to share our latest project...

BabyHex



BabyHex was created to emulate the human language learning process over time and through real-time interaction with caregivers and teachers.

Built in collaboration with child developmental psychologists, she is the product of meticulous design. Her virtual learning draws on the latest research in psycholinguistics.

She is now in our Beta phase of development, and we are calling on (legally consenting) participants such as yourself to help us train the system.

Engage BabyHex through:

1) IMITATION - Choose simple words to say aloud for her to practice repeating. Ensure your audio is enabled.

2) ASSOCIATION – Hold up an image or object for her to identify (be sure to align it with her HexCam).

3) GENERATION – Initiate her motor babble sequence; praise emergent syntactical coherence.

4) CALIBRATION - Adjust the rate of her learning so that her output follows language use in a consistent and realistic progression.

[note: BabyHex is not yet compatible with mobile devices]
