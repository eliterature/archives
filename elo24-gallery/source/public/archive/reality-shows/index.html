REALITY SHOWS

In times of constant crisis there is an urgent need for innovative forms of writing that can adequately represent the complexities of the current climate emergency. Traditional narratives often struggle to convey the multifaceted nature of climate change, which involves a multitude of interconnected actors enmeshed with one another across vastly variable scales of time and space. This proposal seeks to explore how hypermedia fiction, with its capacity to create linkages between disparate elements, can serve as a platform for representing these intricate interactions. By leveraging the unique capabilities of hypermedia, we can develop a more comprehensive understanding of the interconnected nature of this complex issue and its wide-ranging implications.

REALITY SHOWS is a hypertext that follows a dystopian reality show franchise as a way to confront the entangled nature of spectacle, globalization, neocolonialism, and climate justice in the Anthropocene. The project seeks to challenge traditional boundaries and power structures, and to highlight the realities that aren’t typically shown on camera.

The narrative is generated by a system of actor-based models that simulate the intertwined interactions between contestants, viewers, corporations, the weather, and the built environment. By giving agency to both human and non-human intelligences, the project seeks to reflect the complex interdependencies that characterize our world. The results of these simulations are then inputted into a generative AI, which outputs the data as natural text that is then formatted into a web-based hypertext. The resulting narrative embraces the chimeric nature of existence, a narrative re-wilding that invites us to imagine new ways of being where the lines between technology, ecology, and society are blurred and redrawn in ways that open up new possibilities for living on this planet.
