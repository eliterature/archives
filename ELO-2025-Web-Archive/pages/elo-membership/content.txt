ELO Memberships To attend the conference, all participants must be registered members of the Electronic Literature Organization (ELO). We offer both one-time memberships and annual memberships ; all pricing is in USD. Several tiers are available to reflect a variety of professional and financial circumstances. Please review the membership options below and choose the one that best fits your needs. We also offer sponsorship tiers for those who wish to further support the organization or sponsor individual participants.
