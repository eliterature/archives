Presenters at "Visionary Landscapes: The Electronic Literature Organization 2008 Conference" are expected to join the ELO by the time of the conference. We offer several levels of membership. Please select the appropriate level and include this information on the Registration Form when registering for the conference. Payments are also accepted through PayPal prior to the conference. Student, part-time, and unaffiliated artist membership, $US 30 per year General Membership, $US 50 per year Sponsor Membership, $US 100 per year Lifetime Membership, $US 2000 We are a member-supported organization and depend upon memberships and contributions to keep working on behalf of electronic literature. Thus, your support is essential in keeping resources such as the ELO Directory and the ELO site available and updated. It also allows us to continue producing publications, putting on readings and conferences, and advocating the literary possibilities of the digital medium. The Electronic Literature Organization is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, and is recognized as a charitable institution by the Internal Revenue Service (Federal Tax ID #36-4314614).
