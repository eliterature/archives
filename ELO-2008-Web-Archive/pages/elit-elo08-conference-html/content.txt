The Electronic Literature Organization 2008 conference will be held 29 May-1 June 2008, in Vancouver, Washington, USA (not to be confused with Vancouver, British Columiba, Canada). The conference hotel is the Hilton Vancouver Washington. More information about the hotel, and how to make reservations at the special conference rate is available on the "Travel / Hotel" page of this website. The special conference room rate is available until Monday, 28 April 2008. All conference participants should make and pay for their own hotel reservations. Conference activities will be held at the Hilton Vancouver Washington (downtown Vancouver), at Clark College (near downtown Vancouver), at Washington State University Vancouver (bus transportation will be provided), and at Northbank Artists Gallery (downtown Vancouver).
