The Electronic Literature Organization 2008 conference will be held in Vancouver, Washington, (not to be confused with Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada), 29 May—1 June 2008. Participants traveling by air should arrive in Portland, Oregon. The Portland International Airport (PDX) offers service from several international, national, and regional air carriers. Cabs and rental cars are available at PDX. Vancouver, Washington, is a short drive across the Columbia River from PDX. Vancouver, Washington, may also be reached via Amtrak Train Service. The train station is a very short cab ride from the conference hotel. Participants may also drive to Vancouver, Washington. Consult your favorite mapping service for the best route. The Hilton Vancouver Washington , located in downtown Vancouver, is the designated conference hotel. Hotel contact information: 301 W. 6th Street Vancouver, Washington USA 98660 1-360-828-4317 Hilton Vancouver Washington web site A block of rooms is reserved for 27 May—1 June 2008. The special conference room rate is available until Monday, 28 April 2008, or until the group block is sold-out, whichever comes first. A dedicated reservation web page is available for the Electronic Literature Organization 2008 conference. Please use this web page to reserve your hotel room(s) at the conference rate.
