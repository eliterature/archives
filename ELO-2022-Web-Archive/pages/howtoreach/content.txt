CONFERENCE VENUE Collegio Gallio Via Tolomeo Gallio, 1 22100 Como (CO) – Italy Phone: +39 031.269302 DESTINATION COMO To reach Como you have to travel in Lombardy, Italy. The closest airports to the main venue are Milan Malpensa, Milan Linate and Bergamo Orio al Serio. Arriving at these airports you will then find different types of transport to get to the conference: trains, buses, car rental and finally, but not recommended, taxis. Some intercontinental flights may also arrive at Rome airports. From there, it is possible to take another flight or a fast train to Milan (Frecciarossa or Italo). If you have a little more time, you can easily explore northern Italy, starting in Lombardy, with one of Europe's greatest capitals, Milan, and its fashion, art and shopping districts. You can then travel by car, train or bus to many other attractions.
