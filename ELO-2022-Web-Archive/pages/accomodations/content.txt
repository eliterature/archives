ELO 2022 ACCOMODATIONS WHERE TO STAY IN COMO Como is a tourist city that hosts many travellers from all over the world. On this page we give you a quick overview of some of the facilities that may be of interest to stay in Como during the Conference. The facilities are all close to the main venue in the city centre, just a few minutes walk away. There are many other accomodations outside the centre, but these require travel by public transport or car hiring. Accomodations close to the main venue You can contact the Hotels/B&amp;Bs listed here to book a room according to your needs. If you contact some of the listed solutions, consider that with the ones marked as “CONVENTIONED” you can mention the ELO 2022 Conference, either by phone or by e-mail, to obtain a more advantageous price than the price list and many online booking sites. New accomodations coming soon VISIT COMO SUGGESTIONS AND TIPS Como is an amazing small city with a lot of interesting surprises to offer. If you want to plan possible visits, excursions, walks, or find interesting sights and places, delicious food or unexpected aperitifs, try the website https://www.visitcomo.eu/ which is full of suggestions and ideas. VISIT NORTHERN ITALY DISCOVER MORE? If you have a little more time, you can easily explore northern Italy, starting in Lombardy, with one of Europe's greatest capitals, Milan, and its fashion, art and shopping districts. You can then travel by car, train or bus to many other attractions.
