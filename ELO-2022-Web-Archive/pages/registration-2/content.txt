ELO 2022 REGISTRATION FORM Registration for “ELO 2022 – E2Lit: Education and Electronic Literature Conference” is now open. The registration form is available at the bottom of this page. In order to participate in the conference, candidates will have to pay 2022 membership dues to the Electronic Literature Organization ($50 regular membership; $25 students &amp; under/unemployed scholars and artists). Click HERE to join the ELO Candidates who have been accepted to the ELO2022 conference or exhibition must register by May 2nd, 2022. The schedule for the conference will not be finalised until after this date, and any submissions by unregistered delegates (including ELO membership dues) will be removed from the program. Due to high demand we have introduced the payment by bank transfer. To pay by bank transfer please proceed in the normal registration process until the final step where you will be asked to click on the paypal button to pay by paypal or credit card. If you do not proceed, you will receive a payment reminder within 24 hours with the bank account reference for registration. For any doubts you can write to info@elo2022.com. REGISTRATION DEADLINE If you have to present a paper/artwork/talk/poster/performance (etc) please complete your registration before May 2, 2022 otherwise your work will not be included in the conference. The Conference is promoted by the Electronic Literature Organization and it will have its principal in-presence (and online) venue in Como (Italy) and 5 more activities in Brazil, Finland, Israel, Japan, and Mexico. The International Conference will offer keynotes, panels with paper presentations in parallel tracks, lightning talks, debates and experimental labs.
