<h1>“Show me how to do this”<br />
Learning E-lit by Making Together</h1>
<h3><b>CFP: ELO 2022 Special Call: Mentoring Exhibition<br />
</b><b>Deadline <del>March 15, 2022</del> April 1, 2022<br />
</b><b>Online Exhibition: June 2022<br />
</b><b>Curated by María Goicoechea and Mark Marino<br />
</b><a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfHlfIiRBHtcXKTL0Y5nQRU8vUDWD-IMo-n9U56xjK8NhaCTA/viewform?usp=sf_link"><b>Submission form:</b></a></h3>
<p>Call for Works</p>
<p>The making of electronic literature is an artisanal practice, born of community practices of sharing. While some artists find their own way into the rabbit holes of e-lit, most follow a guide or teacher, learning as a sorcerer’s apprentice.  Whether this happens in a formal setting, like a classroom, or in an informal context, like a rainy day passtime for a family, the time spent sharing the forms and tools of electronic literature as well as techniques for making e-lit is crucial to bring new artists into the field and new works into being. In that way, the process is very much like teaching other forms of craft, like sewing, or even cooking, which has in turn inspired e-lit, such as the field of gastropoetics.</p>
<p>For this exhibition, we seek works that have emerged out of such tutelage and collaboration, where a mentor, teacher, or parent has introduced one or more new artists to the field by making a work of electronic literature with them.</p>
<p>Works will be exhibited in an online exhibition as part of the ELO 2022 hybrid conference.  We will also plan an online launch either during or before the conference.</p>
<p>Given the pedagogical emphasis of #ELOitalia and the setting of the in-person conference in a K-12 school, any  works aimed at or appropriate for younger audiences are especially welcomed. This exhibit will have a special section for works aimed at middle grade and young adult audiences.</p>
<p>We are calling for all genre of works, including:</p>
<p>interactive fiction, poetry generators, hypertext, Third Generation E-Lit (including works on Instagram, Twitter, and TikTok), VR/AR/XR, et cetera.</p>
<p>Note: Works already submitted to ELO 2022 that fit the exhibition criteria will be considered. If you submit a work that you have already submitted to the conference, just indicate that in your application.</p>
<h2><strong>Various details:</strong></h2>
<p>At least the submitting author (or mentor) will be required to have a current ELO Membership (minimal unaffiliated member $25) to have their work exhibited. That membership fee does not include ELO 2022 registration.</p>
<p>By March 15, use <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfHlfIiRBHtcXKTL0Y5nQRU8vUDWD-IMo-n9U56xjK8NhaCTA/viewform?usp=sf_link">this form</a> to send us:</p>
